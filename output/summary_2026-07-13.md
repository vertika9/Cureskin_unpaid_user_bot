**Summary:** This week's audit covered 3,312 total bot exchanges, of which 145 (4.4%) were non-answers routed to doctor-call flows and 22 timed out. Of the 3,145 answered exchanges, a random sample of 1000 was evaluated: the bot was rated unsatisfactory on 9.7% of responses (95% CI ±1.83%). The most frequent failure modes were vague or generic responses that did not address the user's specific concern, misunderstood questions (particularly in Hindi/Hinglish), and cases where the bot failed to acknowledge affordability concerns or specific product questions. Notable failure patterns:

- "Bot claims sunscreen prevents tanning and dark spots, but this is misleading—sunscreen prevents further damage but won't reverse existing pigmentation. User's concern (sunscreen causing dark spots) wasn't directly addressed."
- "User asked "Hindi me likhona" (write in Hindi), but bot responded in Hinglish about face washes—ignored the actual request and answered an unrelated question."

- "Misleading claim that "face can recover in one week" without addressing individual variation; response is generic and doesn't directly answer the user's implicit question about timeline realism."
